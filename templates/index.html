Welcome to Melee Randomizer!


An epic video showcasing the randomizer will be placed here, once it's ready.


Melee Randomizer is an application that lets you create a unique and randomly modded version of Melee in just a few clicks. There are also many customization options to let you play Melee the way you want to! The goal is for the standard settings to remain balanced enough that any seed could be played in a competitive tournament. Players who load the same seed up into an emulator are able to play together online. Sharing a seed is as simple as copying the link of the seed page and pasting it somewhere where people can see it. On the desktop version, one simply copys the flagset and has their friends paste it into their app. Melee Randomizer supports a Discord server community with weekly tournaments where players study a seed in advance to discover the meta and use their knowledge to crush the competition!
